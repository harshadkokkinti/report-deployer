Finding 02

Returning guest notes room quality decline.

From the review
“This has been my 5th visit to the resort. I'm grading the stay on the scale of price, based off my most recent stay only. Most of what I have to say would be nit picky for a regular hotel stay but not really excusable when you're paying $700/night. The good: This hotel always has the most friendly staff around. I have never had any issues with the attentiveness or the communication of the staff. Everyone from the Front desk, Housekeeping, Spa Desk and even groundskeepers were extremely welcoming and polite. The location is out of this world, tucked away just outside of the San Juan Mountains the site offers beautiful views and an experience unlike anything I've had. The spa events are a really cool addition that I don't recall experiencing before this stay. Included in the price they have multiple activities throughout the day to enhance your spa and wellness experience. We tried the mud bath, sound yoga, and sauna classes and found all to be really enjoyable. The room was a decent size, We reserved the double-double beds each had their own private bedroom with a spacious common area, kitchenette and decent sized bathroom. The bad: We reserved the room for 3 guests, This was included in the description of the reservation and was known by the resort evidenced by the fact that they did provide 3 robes and 3 beach towels in the room. However, the sets of 3 stopped there. Provided with the room was only one set of shampoo, lotion, soap, coffee/tea, drinks in the fridge etc. For the price, I think it's a bit stingy to only offer 1oz of shampoo per room for 3 guests on a 2 night stay. Especially knowing that their mineral pools might need some additional shampoo to rinse out our hair. Our soaps/shampoos were never refilled after housekeeping dropped by. The towels left on the floor were never picked up or replaced by housekeeping after the first night. The rooms are getting a bit dated at this point. We were placed in the "Deluxe" building which was once a part of the older half of the resort. The room was...fine..after serving years and years of guests coming from the hot springs dripping mineral water everywhere I suppose that a permanent sulfur smell could be expected but it's just greater evidence of the rooms age. Again, for the price I'd say the room is getting a bit dated.”
Jul 9 2026 Chris Yoakem Room Quality

Finding 04

Loyal guests praise staff by name repeatedly.

From the review
“Five stars all around. Christina Martinez was absolutely wonderful and made our stay smooth, relaxing, and completely stress free. I accidentally booked our reservation for August instead of July, which is the kind of travel mistake that can send you into an instant panic. Thankfully, Christina quickly figured out what happened and moved our reservation with no hassle at all. She seriously saved us. She gave us a full rundown of the resort, handed us a map, answered every question, and made sure we knew the pools, tubs, and springs are open 24/7 for overnight guests. She also encouraged us to check out the resort's Wellness Activities, and I'm so glad she did. Christina is truly one of the kindest and sweetest people we've met. The resort itself is incredible. The rooms are beautiful, the views are amazing, and there are more than 50 pools, hot springs, and soaking tubs to explore. You can easily spend hours finding your favorites. You can also spend almost your entire stay in a comfy robe. It's optional, but honestly, I highly recommend it. It makes the whole experience feel like a true spa getaway from start to finish. The Wellness Activities were one of the biggest highlights of our stay. They offer experiences throughout the day and evening, and many of them are included. My wife and I attended the Evening Renewal Mud Ritual with Jess and Betty, and it was such a fun and unique experience. The mud comes from the Mojave Desert and is mixed with local hot springs water to create what can only be described as magical mud. After rinsing off, we followed Jess's recommendation and attended the Aroma Sauna inside the gorgeous Panoramic Sauna. It was incredibly relaxing, and the views overlooking the springs and town were beautiful. Right after the sauna, we did the Cold Bucket Rinse. It sounds a little wild, but it was amazing. You feel incredible afterward. We also grabbed a few mixed drinks during our stay, and every single one was excellent. At one point we ordered a panini from the grill, and it absolutely hit the spot. My wife and I couldn't stop talking about how good it was. Sometimes it's the simple things that end up surprising you the most. Great rooms, great food and drinks, amazing Wellness Activities, outstanding service, and beautiful views. The Springs Resort has everything you need for a relaxing and memorable getaway. We can't recommend it enough! Christina, thanks for making it such a memorable stay!”
Jul 27 2026 Felipe Guzman Staff Recognition
