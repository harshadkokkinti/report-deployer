Finding 01

Bedbugs in Room 317 ignored by staff

From the review
“Absolutely Disgusting - Room 317 Has Bedbugs and They're Still Renting It Out I stayed here this past weekend for a conference, and it turned into an absolute nightmare. Room 317 is infested with bedbugs - and unbelievably, the hotel is still renting it out. At first, the room looked fine - older but seemingly clean. The bed was sagging in the middle, definitely past its prime, but the sheets looked crisp and white. The next morning, after showering, I looked down and both of my legs were covered in bites from my ankles halfway up my shins. I rushed to the front desk, showed the gentleman my legs, and told him the room had bedbugs. He didn't apologize or show any concern - just silently handed me a new room key. When I asked what to do about the clothes I slept in, he simply pointed toward the laundry room. That was it. I had to quickly move all of my belongings myself just to make it to my conference on time. When I went back one last time to grab the rest of my things, a housekeeper was stripping the bed. I told her, "That room has bedbugs." She replied, "I see no evidence of bedbugs." I showed her my legs, covered in bites, and she glanced down, said "hmm," and walked away - total disregard for what had just happened, despite the evidence literally right in front of her. They simply changed the sheets and prepared the room for the next guest. Absolutely appalling. The second room they moved me to was even worse - poor layout, only two working outlets, and a TV on the side wall instead of in front of the bed. My coworker managed to get the room comped, but that did nothing for me since it was paid by my employer. This hotel is filthy, negligent, and completely indifferent to guest health and safety. Avoid this place at all costs.”

Finding 04

Staff champions earn 140 five-star reviews

From the review
“I have made several trips to Huntsville over the years, and while there are plenty of options in town, I often find myself returning to the same trusted few hotels. Although this hotel isn't the newest property in the area, it has been thoughtfully updated, is consistently clean, reasonably priced, and sits in a prime location near the U.S. Space & Rocket Center - a huge bonus for both business and leisure travelers. From the moment I checked in, the tone was set. Timothy welcomed me with a genuine smile and easy conversation, asking about my travels and making sure I had a restful stay ahead. It wasn't just routine front-desk courtesy - it felt personal. He made sure my room was clean and feather-free as requested, which I truly appreciated after a long day on the road. MaKayla is always a joy to see at the front desk. She greets guests with warmth and remembers her regulars, which makes returning feel a bit like coming home. Even when you're asking for something to be corrected or adjusted, the staff handle it with such gracious Southern hospitality - regardless of where they're from - that you can't help but feel valued. Though I didn't see Ms. Edneva during this review, her presence is clearly felt. As one of the managers, she takes pride in maintaining a clean hotel and fostering an atmosphere of kindness and hospitality. She truly embodies that welcoming Southern spirit. The M Club was another highlight. Kimberly was busy making sure guests had everything they needed, yet she still took time to chat warmly with everyone. Her joyful energy - and lovely Bahamian accent - made the space feel lively and welcoming. Even while juggling multiple responsibilities, she ensured the snack area was tidy and well-stocked. Breakfast was just as pleasant. Alley was the only familiar face I recognized, and as always, she was kind and attentive, clearly taking pride in serving guests well. Chef Snoop made it a point to step out as time permitted to ensure everyone was satisfied with the food. When I asked for a bowl of grits, he personally brought it out to me. That small gesture spoke volumes. What stands out most about this hotel isn't flashy dcor or ultra-modern finishes - it's the people. The rooms and food areas are kept clean, the updates are appreciated, the rates are fair, and the location is excellent. But it's the warmth, pride, and consistent hospitality from the staff that keeps me coming back. If you're visiting Huntsville and want a clean, comfortable stay with genuinely caring service, this hotel is a solid choice.”
