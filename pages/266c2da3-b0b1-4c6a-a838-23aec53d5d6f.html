“I'm from North Carolina where Bar-b-Que is practically a religion. The raging debate in the Carolina's is Western verses Eastern BBQ. Eastern is served either plain or with a vinegar and pepper based sauce. Western has a tomato based sweet/spicy sauce. Both are delicious and, to me, MUST be slow smoked with hickory wood. Hickory Park knocks it out of the park on both styles! The meat has a definite hickory kick and is smoked to tender perfection. We tried pork, chicken and beef, with red sauce and without any sauce, and all were outstanding. They do not offer a vinegar based sauce but the flavor of the meat was awesome even plain. The service was friendly and fast. The prices are very reasonable. A large chopped sandwich with a side is less than $9.00. A generous plate with sliced pork, beef, chicken and 2 sides was $16.00. No one ate everything and we have enough left over for a late night snack. One of our party cannot eat gluten. They offered a generous selection gluten free. and the manager came to our table to discuss the menu and explain various options. Get a great meal at a fair price at Hickory Park!”
