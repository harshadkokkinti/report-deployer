Finding 01

Unanswered safety complaint needs attention

From the review
“We recently stayed at this resort and, while parts of the experience were enjoyable, a serious guest safety failure overshadowed the entire trip. During our stay, my wife, who is seven months pregnant, entered the water from the resort's beach under conditions that did not appear unusually hazardous at the time. While in the water, she was struck by a large wave and fell hard onto her hip. As a precaution, we sought medical care, which resulted in $1,585 USD in out-of-pocket expenses. The incident was observed by resort staff, who came over immediately, confirming that it was visible and recognized at the time. However, there was limited assistance or structured response provided following the incident. After returning to the resort, a red flag was raised on the beach indicating hazardous water conditions. We were informed by staff that the flag is normally posted under those conditions but was missed earlier that morning. This is not a minor issue. The presence or absence of a red flag is a primary safety control used to warn guests of dangerous surf. The failure to post it in a timely manner directly impacted our decision to enter the water. While the beach may be considered public, the resort actively manages the beachfront experience, including the use of safety flags, guest seating, and staff presence. Once those controls are in place, there is a reasonable expectation that hazard warnings are monitored and deployed consistently. Equally concerning, following the incident, there was no wheelchair or adequate means available to assist my pregnant wife from our cabin to the front of the property, requiring her to walk a considerable distance after injury. We reported the incident to management, provided full documentation of medical expenses, and made multiple attempts to follow up. To date, the resort has not provided any substantive response or acknowledgment beyond initial receipt. While the location itself is beautiful, guest safety, hazard communication, and incident response should be baseline expectations at any resort. Based on this experience, I would strongly encourage others to exercise caution, particularly regarding ocean conditions, and not rely solely on the resort's safety indicators.”

Finding 02

Food consistency noted by constructive guest

From the review
“We stayed at Keyonna Beach Resort for 9 nights and had a wonderful experience. The resort is beautiful, peaceful, and exactly what we were looking for. A special thank you to Tevin, who made our stay even better. His beach service was exceptional. He was friendly, attentive, and always fun to talk to. He was a major highlight of our trip. We stayed in Room 49, which has incredible views. The plunge pool was fantastic, and the beach is absolutely stunning. The water is crystal clear, and there are plenty of fish to see while snorkeling on both the left and right sides of the beach. The staff throughout the resort were genuinely kind and welcoming, and they were always happy to accommodate menu substitutions. We also enjoyed the rotating dinner menu. Our main feedback is around consistency. Food and drinks could vary quite a bit from day to day. For example, we ordered two iced coffees just 10 minutes apart, and one arrived with no milk or sugar while the other came with milk and a large amount of sugar. We also ordered the same burger several times during our stay, and on one occasion it was prepared completely differently. Occasionally, menu items also arrived differently than described or with unavailable ingredients that weren't mentioned beforehand. There was one exception to the otherwise excellent service during our final breakfast. My wife requested that her pancakes be served without the fruit compote due to a mild fruit allergy. When they arrived with the compote, she politely mentioned the mistake. The order was corrected, but the server seemed visibly frustrated afterward, and that demeanor continued for the rest of the meal, even during simple requests like asking for more coffee. It stood out because every other interaction during our stay was so warm and friendly. Overall, we had a great experience, and we hope this feedback helps make an already beautiful resort even better.”
