The bros at the podium in the lobby will hold your bags for you, for a cash fee, and they won't even tell you about the Yobot that stores bags for free. The rooftop deck is on the 4th floor, which is practically street-level, and is only accessible if you order something, or pay $20+ per person for an outdoor movie of a years-old movie you could probably catch on the little TV in your room. It is a cute TV with a rainbow frame of light around it. Oooh pretty. Also, if you do venture out onto that deck, maybe wear a helmet. You are below twenty floors of rooms with windows that open over the deck, where phones and other things could easily slip off a desk or bed and out the window, onto your head. The "amenities" are all listed on a website that includes a big spread of fun things to do in NYC. There is no "here's what is physically right here in this hotel" sign, document, page, or even verbal explanation at the front desk. Like, where are the ice machines? You are handed a business card with a QR code, which is supposed to serve, I guess, as the facility map, but again, it goes to a site that is so expansive that it is really difficult to narrow it down to the features that the resort fee is allegedly covering. The gym is a single hotel room with a glass door and as many pieces of equipment as can be squeezed into a NYC hotel room. I'm not a gym person so IDC, but if you care, find pics. Broadway shows and city tours are not in the building. Even the things that ARE in the building are listed by their street address, so you're simultaneously mapping them to figure out which of these things might be down on the 4th floor. The TV has a sign about ordering food from a certain channel - but it's just a TV channel. No working ice machines within several floors of our floor. So So So much attitude from the restaurant downstairs that claims to "not be affiliated with the hotel" when you ask them for ice due to the absence of working ice machines. And yet, your resort fee is allegedly covering access to the deck where that unaffiliated restaurant operates There is a massive air gap at the top of the bathroom door (about 8 inches - not a construction error gap) which allows for real comedy when your family of four is all together in the room. Do you like your travel companions to hear the splash of the toilet when you use it? No? Then book somewhere else. Your kids will be amused, for free, so if you're with family on a budget, free comedy show! If there is bathroom stank, there is room stank. The hotel won't take cash payments, so I saw a couple turned away who had planed to pay for their trip in cash. Watch out for the surprise fees, which they claim are clear, and yet so many people find surprising. Any other business would reflect on those reviews and work to make changes to improve the guest experience, but it is clear that this hotel has no intention of working on their messaging. When I expressed shock at some lack of basic hotel information (like a map or a list or something basic), the person at the front desk asked me if I had ever stayed in a hotel before. Yay for customer service! It is smelly, run-down, low-tech, dated, and moldy, and it is staffed by people who hate their jobs and hate the guests, but it has a great view and is in a great location.
