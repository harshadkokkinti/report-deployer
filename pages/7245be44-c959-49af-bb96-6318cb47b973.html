“Overall, our stay at the Arizona Grand Resort was okay, but it didn't meet my expectations, especially considering the price. The highlight of our trip was definitely the Oasis Water Park. The pools were clean, well-maintained, and our family had a great time there. The staff we interacted with were friendly, and the water park was the best part of the resort. Unfortunately, there were several disappointing aspects of our stay. The suite itself was not very clean upon arrival, and there was a constant rotten egg smell throughout the room that resembled old plumbing or sewer gas. It was unpleasant and took away from what should have been a relaxing stay. The pull-out sofa bed was also extremely uncomfortable. After only a few hours of sleeping on it, it caused significant back pain. For a resort that caters to families, I expected much better accommodations for additional guests. I also expected more dining options for a resort of this size. The food choices felt limited, especially for guests staying multiple days. Movie night was a nice idea, but the screen was far too small for the number of families attending. A larger screen would make the experience much more enjoyable. My biggest concern, however, was a serious safety issue that was never addressed. There were large shards of broken glass scattered on the stairs near the Camelback area leading to the lobby. We reported it to staff multiple times because it posed a clear danger, especially for children and anyone wearing sandals. Despite notifying staff on several occasions, the glass remained there for days, forcing guests to walk through the area. It was very disappointing to see such an obvious hazard go unaddressed.”
