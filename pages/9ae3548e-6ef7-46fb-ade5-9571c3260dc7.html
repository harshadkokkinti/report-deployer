Finding 01

Wedding reception ruined by broken promises

From the review
“READ THIS BEFORE BOOKING EVENTS OR ROOMS WITH THIS HOTEL My husband and I celebrated our wedding reception in this hotel. The staff were lovely and gave us the best service. The bartender was phenomenal and the chef was amazing. However, we wanted to let express several concerns that impacted our overall experience: 1. We were promised two microphones but one of them did not work, which negatively impacted our evening, and was duly noticed as my two dear friends who flew all the way from Hong Kong to sing the song they prepared for me on our first dance. On a night so important and special to us, we would hope that they all would ensure that all the components of the evening flowed smoothly. 2. We were also promised reservations at Charmaine's after the event but upon arrival, the whole place was not available. The lights and the noises for the night were constantly remarked on by guests, negatively coloring their experience of one of the most important nights of our lives. I even gave Dean a follow up email on that matter but he never replied back. 3. On Friday night, the day after the wedding, me and my family encountered a deeply disappointing situation with the hotel front desk. The key card my mother in law was using for her room stopped working, a regular fixture in the hospitality industry, and she went to the front desk staff to have it fixed. The hotel staff immediately began harassing and questioning her. After a hard day of traveling, this not only stressed out my mother in law who was suffering from a health condition, in severe pain, and needed her medication from her room but was unable to retrieve it. The concierge caused her even further harm by stating that her medicine and bags were removed from her room, implying she would have to replace the over $8000 in goods and products she had with her. If this were not enough, my husband and I had to change our plans and leave our post-wedding celebration just to go back to the hotel and confirm our reservation, an inane and needless action, considering if the concierge used the means available to them to properly conduct their job instead of inappropriately profiling my loved ones. These issues caused considerable frustration during what should have been the happiest days of our lives. I reached out to the head of front Louie, head of sales Patti, and head of events Dean and received no word, apology, or even responses from some of them. I have been trying to reach out for the past few weeks on the issue, seeking some sort of resolution, but I was just being gaslit to think that the whole situation was our own to blame, that we went to the hotel and had to abandon our plans because we were the ones who decided to leave despite the front desk requiring physical ID even after check in, that our mother in law overreacted to the whole situation despite her medical condition, that somehow the keycard demagnetizing was due to our mother in law's devices despite the design of the technology being an oversight on their part. It is truly unfortunate that all of this had to happen on our special day, and it is even more disappointing that instead of receiving reassurance or even respect from the staff, our concerns were simply dismissed and forever colored our memories of what was supposed to be a beautiful wedding weekend filled love and happiness.”

Returning customer notes worn quality

From the review
“I stayed at the Proper when it first opened - I actually just edited my review from 8 years ago to write this one. The neighborhood was manageable then, but today, it is genuinely terrifying. To be fair, the hotel's interior is nice and offers a safe haven once you are inside. This said , for the premium prices Marriott charges, parts of the rooms are starting to feel noticeably worn and not up to the standard the hotel used to hold. Dealbreaker though, the location. The immediate surrounding area is an open-air drug market with active drug use, severe homelessness, and incredibly unsanitary conditions right outside the doors. You simply cannot safely walk around the area, whether it is day or night. I tried to walk to find dinner the first night and had to retreat back to the hotel and order delivery. It is shocking that Marriott charges such high rates for a property where guests essentially cannot safely leave the building on foot.
