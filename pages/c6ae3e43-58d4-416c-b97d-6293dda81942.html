Finding 01

Shower head fell on guest with no reply.

From the review
“I rarely leave negative reviews, but I feel other travelers should know what to expect. We chose The Manhattan at Times Square Hotel because of its location, but unfortunately the experience did not justify the price. This is an expensive hotel, and for what we paid, we could have stayed at a much nicer hotel in Manhattan with better accommodations and significantly better service. Our stay got off to a rough start. When we arrived, the hotel's computer systems were down. The lobby was overcrowded, chaotic, and completely disorganized. Guests were standing around with no direction, little communication, and no effort to make an already frustrating situation more comfortable. Even offering bottled water or keeping guests informed would have shown some level of hospitality. We also overheard another guest saying they had been assigned a room that was already occupied. After finally checking in, we encountered a serious safety issue. While my daughter was taking a shower, the shower head fell on her. We immediately reported it, but no one offered to move us to another room. I had to request a room change myself, which didn't happen until after midnight. Maintenance came to inspect the room, but the issue was never resolved before we moved. The room itself was disappointing. It was cold, poorly insulated, and we could hear street noise throughout the night. Every morning, construction or drilling began around 7:00 a.m., making it impossible to sleep. To make matters worse, only two of the hotel's six elevators were working, resulting in long waits throughout the day. The breakfast was another disappointment. It is advertised in a way that suggests a continental breakfast, but what you actually receive is a pastry, one piece of fruit, and coffee or juice. Considering the additional cost, the value simply isn't there. The employee working the breakfast area was consistently rude, unfriendly, and seemed completely uninterested in providing good customer service. I do want to recognize the employees who made a positive impression. Steven, the manager, was kind and professional. The bell service staff were excellent, and two members of the front desk team were genuinely helpful and courteous. They deserve recognition. Unfortunately, those positive interactions could not make up for the overall experience. Between the outdated rooms, maintenance problems, elevator issues, constant noise, misleading breakfast, and lack of hospitality, this hotel simply does not provide the level of quality you would expect for the price. If I were spending this amount of money again, I would absolutely stay somewhere else. There are far better hotels in Manhattan for the same price. I cannot recommend this property.”
Blanca Magallanes Jul 24 2026 Unanswered Crisis

Finding 02

Returning guest notes decline since 2013.

From the review
“A friend booked for our spring trip. We had stayed here in 2013 and had a good experience then, but the hotel has clearly gone downhill and has been neglected since then. I was concerned about the cleanliness of the room after reading the reviews, but will say the room was sufficiently cleaned. Beyond that...not a very relaxing stay. The tub had a space beneath the tub and the base, so you could feel water collecting and squishing in a pocket when you showered. Our AC wasn't working the first night, and, as others have noted, the thermostat was not even connected to the wall and came right off. They did, however, send someone to fix it while we were out of the room the first morning. As others have said, the walls are incredibly thin, so be prepared to hear every word of your neighbors conversations. One of our neighbors also decided to smoke in their room and set off a smoke alarm at 5:30 in the morning. There were a lot of loud vibrating noises from what sounded like air moving through the plumbing, which was very disruptive. The beds are also very outdated with uncomfortable mattresses. We did stay in one of the old un-renovated rooms, so the refreshed rooms may be better. But we are not planning to stay here again. Two stars because the service was good, especially when getting our AC issue fixed, and the staff in the lobby were very friendly, greeting with a smile when we were coming in or out during the day.”
