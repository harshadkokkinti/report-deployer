Finding 01

Bugs in food at Hartley Restaurant goes unanswered.

From the review
“THERE WERE BUGS IN THE FOOD! ! I booked the Panoramic King Suite for my husband's birthday with the stay and dine package that included a prepaid $50 credit for the Hartley Restaurant. We went down for breakfast this morning and caught the brunch buffet. About 5-10 minutes into eating, I started eating the baked egg white muffins. I realized there were small white/yellowish and black bugs crawling under the egg bite and onto my plate once I flipped the muffin over. The waitress brought the chef out and he asked if we wanted him to cook us a steak or something but at that point we didn't want a THING from the kitchen. The restaurant manager came over and was trying to make it seem as though the "ingredients,like the fruit, are organic that there may have been reason." But, my husband wasn't having any of that because organic or not, there should be NO bugs on my plate or crawling in my food! Not only that, but the bugs were not coming from the fruit. They were coming from the eggs! We went to check out immediately. Upon checking out the hotel manager, Johnathan, never acknowledged us personally. They ended up giving us half off our stay but when we asked the receptionist about the returning the $50 dining credit, she stated it would "just be a loss" to my husband and I. Of course we disagreed and the hotel manager, Johnathan, finally acknowledged us but had the audacity to have an attitude about us wanting the prepaid $50 credit back. "That was for the food you ate", is what he said and I pushed back by letting him know that the food was the incident! Why would I pay for food with bugs in it?!. Then Johnathan rudely told the receptionist "JUST TAKE OFF THE PARKING VALET" and walked off like we ruined his shift or something. No apology, no accountability of what happened, just let us leave as if we were the problem. I will let everyone I know do not come to this hotel but more importantly, don't order anything from the Hartley! We will be reporting them to the health department and restaurant code enforcement first thing Monday morning.”

Finding 02

Loyal guest explores other options after multiple issues.

From the review
“We started staying here a few years back because my husband's main office for his firm is walking distance to this hotel. He comes here several times a year and I accompany him a few times when there are social events. It has a decent gym and I can walk to Atlantic Station. We checked in yesterday and were pretty surprised to see the beautiful shower but not one ledge or shelf holding the large bottles of soap, shampoo and conditioner. They were lined up on the shower floor in the corner. I went to blow dry my hair this morning and the hairdryer was broken. Luckily our event is tomorrow and I am currently waiting for a replacement hairdryer to be delivered. I just called and offered to pick it up at the front desk and my offer was declined and I was told the staff must be busy doing something else and she would call them again. My husband had to coopt a blowdryer from a maid cleaning another room. When I finally got a working blowdryer, the socket in the bathroom is dead. No way to reset the GFCI electrical outlet. I had to blow dry my hair in the hallway and use the coin tray by the full size mirror as my shelf for my brushes. Additionally, the room either above or near us had music blaring last night. It was shut down once but about 15 minutes later the nonsense commenced. We called the front desk and it ceased for the rest of the evening. I am usually not quick to pull the plug on a business but given that all these issues arose in one visit, we are exploring other hotel options for our additional visits to the area. We visit Atlanta a lot, as do my husband's work colleagues.”
