Finding 01

Unanswered 1-star cites food illness and noise

From the review
“I stayed at the most expensive villa at Kayon Jungle Resort for 3 nights. Let me give an honest review by an overnight guest, starting with the positives. The location is lovely and peaceful. It backs on to a jungle. The place itself is a cool design that nicely uses the hillside and blends into the flora. The staff are accommodating and attentive although often times disorganized. Food variety and quality is hit and miss. The gym is great. Downsides, which are many: The place is 95% Korean guests. I didn't get the memo. I'd rather not feel like an outsider anywhere except to locals. People from outside are permitted in to get drunk poolside, and they do. Apparently by being "adults only" the adults give themselves permission to act like children. I presumed it meant that people can expect far higher levels of peace. The resort cranks loud thumping dance music or has amplified live bands during the day. If you think you are coming to a peaceful jungle, you're not, these people are shaking the jungle with music. You will hear it from the restaurants, the spa, and your room. It ruined our spa treatments. I had dinner at the restaurant on my first night and got sick the next morning. That knocked me out for 24 hours. Dishes were good about 40% of the time. Having a wide variety of food means much is frozen and pre-made, like the pizza, which was terrible. The villa was in terrible shape. The floors were creaky and crunchy so badly that you would wake up anyone sleeping by stepping softly anywhere. The rooms are too humid and smell of mildew. The choice to offer zero privacy in the bathroom is uncomfortable. Internet speeds falter badly. A huge amounts of repair and maintenance is needed. I'll stop there. I won't return to this resort or any of their Kayon sister resorts. I am profoundly disappointed to have landed at party central and I would strongly discourage anyone seeking a peaceful holiday from booking this resort.”

Finding 04

Staff warmth earns a stellar 4.8 rating

From the review
“My husband and I chose The Kayon Jungle Resort for our delayed honeymoon, and we are so glad we did! It was our first time staying in a luxury resort, and it was extremely luxury. Room: we stayed in the Valley View Villa and it was spacious, clean, beautiful, and had a HEATED plunge pool with a tranquil vibe that we spent hours in. Food: I was obsessed with the breakfast. You could order anything off the menu (and as much as you liked) plus as much coffee and juice as you liked. The jungle valley view from the breakfast was amazing. Lunch and breakfast also had a really extensive international menu - although my favourite was the Balinese and Indonesian menu :). Service: incredible! During our stay we primarily interacted with our two butlers Nik and Risky. They were both so friendly and helpful and immediately responded when we had any request. Our driver Nova was also extremely skilful at navigating Ubud streets and made our journey comfortable! Thank you also to all the other amazing staff who were unfailingly friendly and helpful. Facilities: the gym was really good for a hotel gym. While it did not contain all the machines we normally use, it had enough for a full-body strength training and cardio. The main Wanna Pool was gorgeous, and during the day it has a fun Instagrammer / party vibe. We paid for the private yoga which was fun, but during our afternoon session the music from Wanna was playing next to the yoga studio, making it hard to hear our instructor and giving a more party yoga vibe ;). Location: the resort is in a gorgeous location overlooking a deep jungle valley. During traffic, it took us around 40mins to get to central Ubud, along small and windy roads. This wasn't an issue for us as we spent most of our time in the resort. However when we tried to get Grab or Gojek the drivers said we were too far away. The hotel itself provides great room service and taxi services - just more expensive than Grab or Gojek would be :). Overall vibe: the resort is beautiful, tranquil, well-maintained and the staff are incredible. As a gay / LGBT couple, we always felt included and supported, and were happy to see other gay couples staying at The Kayon also. If you would like to splurge, I highly recommend The Kayon Jungle Resort!”
Service Staff Hayden Blain
