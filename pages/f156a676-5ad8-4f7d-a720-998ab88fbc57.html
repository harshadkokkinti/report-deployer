Finding 01

Guest reports eggshells in omelet and staff misconduct

From the review
“I've vacationed in many hotels over the years, but I honestly don't remember an experience as bad as the one we had at Sousse. Our expectations were much higher for a hotel in this price and segment, but we were disappointed in almost every aspect of our stay. When we went down for breakfast, most of the tables were dirty and unmade. The situation was even worse at dinner; we couldn't find a single table, so we stood with the waiters waiting for someone to get up. And we weren't the only ones; many guests were waiting for a table in the same way. In terms of service, we found the staff and chefs' attention in the main restaurant, snack areas, and bars to be extremely inadequate. You sit down at a table, and you're practically scrambling to get served. Most of the staff seemed very unhappy and unmotivated. At one point, I even jokingly said to a staff member, "Come on, give me a couple of slaps so you can relax"; that's how tense and unmotivated the atmosphere was. The food quality was also absolutely unworthy of a hotel of this caliber. In the evening, you might find plenty of sugar pastries and walnuts in the dessert section; However, when a tray of pistachio baklava was served, you could grab it if you were lucky; we didn't see any more when it ran out. It was also surprising to find items like chips and cheese at dinner. We found eggshells in the omelet we ordered for breakfast. We felt nauseous several times when hard pieces of shells came between our teeth, and we couldn't eat it. It's unacceptable for even a simple omelet to be served like this at a hotel like this. We didn't like the hamburgers and pizzas in the snack section at all. The hamburger, in particular, didn't feel like it came from a quality hotel kitchen; it felt like a reheated, ready-made product from the supermarket. It was far below our expectations in terms of taste and product quality. Finding a sun lounger by the pool was another struggle. You wander around in the sun looking for a lounger because you can't find one. Paying such high prices and then having to chase after tables, service, and sun loungers during your vacation is truly exhausting. One of the things that bothered us most was witnessing some staff members using foul language loudly in areas where guests, women, and children were present. Furthermore, we were quite disturbed by the impression that some bar staff were trying to communicate with female guests in ways that went beyond professional boundaries. I would expect a much higher standard of staff behavior at a facility of this caliber. The strangest part was that we couldn't find a responsible person who would actually address the issue and whom we could properly explain the situation to and request a solution from during our stay. Reading some very positive reviews here, especially those highlighting staff names, frankly makes it difficult to reconcile our own experience. Unfortunately, our experience was the complete opposite. In short, we experienced great disappointment in terms of food quality, service, staff attentiveness, restaurant organization, sun lounger problems, and overall service. Paying this much money and having to chase after tables, sun loungers, and service throughout the holiday is not a "luxury holiday" for me; it's a waste of money. You could get similar service at a much more affordable, even 3-star, facility for this price. At this price point, there are much higher-segment alternatives with much better service quality and guest satisfaction than Susesi. Based on our experience, I definitely do not recommend it at these prices; I suggest you look at alternative hotels. I have stayed in different hotels for years; Susesi was unfortunately one of the worst hotel experiences I have ever had. I would not choose it again.”

Finding 02

Returning guest reports drastic quality decline

From the review
“I stayed at Susesi for 7 nights and 8 days between July 30th and August 6th, my third stay, this time with my father. First of all, I must say that my first two visits (summers of 2019 and 2020) were extremely enjoyable; I experienced the "luxury" in the hotel's name in every sense, and despite the pandemic, I didn't experience the slightest problem. This was the biggest reason why I chose it again for the third time. It was the first place that came to mind without considering any other hotel, and I had complete confidence in it. Unfortunately, it was a huge disappointment. The first negative point started with the room. The room they called a sea view room was actually a room that you might call a partial sea view in other hotels; the balcony wasn't positioned in a way that would allow you to see the sea immediately upon stepping out. Moreover, I had mentioned this to the tour company I booked with. Thankfully, when we expressed our problem, they quickly arranged another room and resolved our issue. Even though we spent our entire holiday on the beach, we sometimes had difficulty finding sunbeds there, let alone talking about the poolside. The constant scrambling for sunbeds and tables is completely at odds with the hotel's "luxury" ideal and makes you feel like you're in a constant race during your vacation. Not to mention the food and/or desserts that might run out at breakfast or dinner if you don't arrive early. The sea, unfortunately, is treated like a stepchild. For example, there's no music playing, unlike by the pool, and apart from beach volleyball or SUP boarding a few days a week, there are no other activities. The beach was clean, though. Trash was collected frequently, so you didn't have to worry about carrying your own trash or where to dispose of it. The hotel is starting to feel outdated. Room cleaning wasn't very good either. My biggest problem and disappointment, accounting for 75%, was the hotel's drastically declining service. In my previous visits, the staff's genuine smiles, their willingness to help, and their happiness while working were evident. Sadly, I experienced the complete opposite this time. The service was practically self-service throughout my stay. Except for a few people, everyone's faces were generally sullen. In addition, hearing from another colleague that your order was taken but wasn't actually placed, as if mocking you, reflects the staff's unhappiness There was also a noticeable decline in the quality and variety of the food. We discussed a few problems we experienced with the reception and they were very helpful, which I appreciate. Generally, they don't turn down complaints or requests, but what disappointed me was that in my previous visits, there wasn't even a need for such a thing. The negative changes in a hotel I once liked have disappointed me. I hope all these problems are resolved and Susesi returns to its former quality.”
