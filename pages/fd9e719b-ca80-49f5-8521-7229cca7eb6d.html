We were told not a single room was available in the hotel for us and we were the last guests to check in. Low and behold, 2 separate guests walked up to the desk after us and had seamless check-ins with full suits to themselves. Finding 04

Loyal customers consistently praise the staff

From the review
“I love this hotel. I come every year around late September for a week. Its central location to the tourist spots makes it easy to walk everywhere. It is also the perfect location for driving up the shore, or going to inland lakes and trails. They recently renovated their rooms and the renovations are very nice and upscale. The service and genuine hospitality of the staff is the real stand out though. There is a real passion to care for and about their guests, no matter who you talk with at the hotel. Don't pass on eating in the bar/restaurant. The chef keeps the menu fresh and seasonal. Currently their braised pork shank with seasonal risotto (butternut squash and apples) is one of the best meals I have had while traveling. If you are fortunate enough to be there when Melissa is the bartender, you will be in for a real treat. Parking is in a garage behind the hotel and requires a short walk through a skyway, down a floor in an elevator to enter the hotel. I believe the current rate is $8/day. This doesn't bother me and its nice to know that my car is in a secure lot. They have updated the entrance protocol to a camera that takes a picture of your license plate to let you in, getting rid of the old ticket system, which is very convenient. The club lounge on the 6th floor is awesome and available 24/7. They serve breakfast and also do a food happy hour as well. There is a stocked fridge with bottled water, sodas, etc and a fresh grind coffee machine that makes amazing coffee drinks. Great value to be in the heart of Duluth!”
Loyal Fans Oct 6 2025 Jeff M
